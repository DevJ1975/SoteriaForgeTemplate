# Safety FORGE 10 Hour Course Shell

Safety FORGE 10 Hour is the first reusable Soteria FORGE compliance-style course pattern. It is a construction-focused, OSHA-aligned shell for hybrid async learning, Vimeo-hosted lessons, quizzes, offline summaries, xAPI records, final assessment, and supervisor practical signoff.

This course is not an official OSHA Outreach Training Program class and does not issue an OSHA/DOL card unless Soteria FORGE later adds an authorized trainer or authorized provider workflow.

## Delivery Pattern

- Public catalog preview exposes outcomes, audience, topic outline, duration, certificate label, and disclaimers.
- Full course content unlocks through marketplace package entitlement.
- Learner delivery uses sequence locking, minimum seat-time metadata, video placeholders, active engagement prompts, quizzes, reflections, and signoff.
- Admin delivery surfaces topic-time targets, lesson count, in-progress learners, completion records, and audit/export readiness.
- Vimeo URLs are placeholders until final videos are authored and linked.

## Module Blueprint

| Module | Minutes | Topic |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Welcome, Course Rules, and Safety FORGE Orientation | 15 | Platform, disclaimer, completion rules, low-bandwidth expectations |
| Introduction to OSHA and Worker Rights | 60 | Rights, responsibilities, reporting, complaints, resources |
| Focus Four: Falls | 90 | Fall hazard recognition, ladders, scaffolds, prevention |
| Focus Four: Electrocution | 45 | Power sources, temporary power, grounding, lockout awareness |
| Focus Four: Struck-By | 45 | Vehicles, dropped objects, equipment, barricades, spotters |
| Focus Four: Caught-In or Between | 45 | Trenches, moving equipment, pinch points, excavations |
| Focus Four Field Lab and Scenario Review | 15 | Hazard sorting and field scenario review |
| PPE and Lifesaving Equipment | 30 | PPE selection, inspection, limits, readiness checklist |
| Health Hazards in Construction | 30 | HazCom, silica, noise, heat, SDS orientation |
| Electives, Final Assessment, and Supervisor Signoff | 225 | Scaffolds, ladders, tools, materials handling, excavation, final assessment, signoff |

Total: 600 minutes.
